# Architecting this "Verify-to-Order" system for a Brew Pub. 

1. The User Experience (The "Happy Flow") 
Instead of a generic QR code that opens a PDF menu, we deploy a Progressive Web

## Potential Friction & Solution 

- The Problem: People might not carry their physical Aadhaar card to a pub. 

- The Solution: 
    Design an Web App to Scan Aadhar QR Scanner and Fetch Details

    1. mAadhaar App: Most young users have this. 
    2. DigiLocker: The scanner can accept DigiLocker QR codes. 
    3. Photos: Many people keep a photo of their Aadhaar on their phone. Our web app can allow Upload from Gallery to scan the QR from a screenshot. 

- Web App:
    - Structure, Easy to scan, Best UI and UX
    - Only Frontend Structure - Minimum / No Backend
    - Best UI/UX handling in mobile, laptop, tablet, iphone screen
    - Easy to select photo from galary quick scan
    - Clear Error Handling and Code Comment
    - Fast Processing Speed and Loading Speed

## App (PWA) flow: 

1. The Hook: Customer sits at the table and scans the Order & Pay QR code. 

2. The Gate: A landing page appears: 
    "To unlock our Craft Beer menu, please verify you are of legal drinking age (21+)." 

3. The Scan: The customer taps "Verify." The browser asks for camera permission. They point their phone at their Aadhaar Card QR code (or mAadhaar app). 

4. The Logic (Local Device): 
The web app scans the QR, extracts the Date of Birth (DOB), and cryptographically validates the UIDAI digital signature to ensure the ID is fake-proof. 

5. The Redirect: 
    o Success (Age > 21): The app redirects to the full menu. 
    o Failure (Age < 21): The app redirects to a Mocktails & Food Only menu. 

## Technical Architecture (OVSE Compliant) 

1. To implement this as an OVSE, our software must handle the Aadhaar Secure QR Code. 

- Offline Logic: The parsing of the QR code happens locally (using client-side 
JavaScript or a localized server in the pub). No data is sent to UIDAI. 

- Signature Validation: Our application holds the public key of UIDAI to verify 
that the QR code was actually generated by the government and not 
Photoshop. 

2. Data Extraction: We extract only two fields: 
    1. DOB: To calculate Age. 
    2. Gender/Pincode: For analytics (optional but valuable). 
    
## 4. Regulatory & Privacy Advantage 

1. This model is safer for the Brew Pub than asking for a physical ID card: 

    - GDPR/DPDP Compliance: We are not storing the image of their ID card (which is dangerous). We are simply running a check and discarding the PII (Personally Identifiable Information).

    - Liability Shield: If police raid the pub, we have a digital audit log: "Table 6  verified as 24 years old at 8:15 PM via UIDAI digitally signed QR." This is a robust legal defense.
